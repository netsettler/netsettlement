This document is my scan of
  Proceedings of the First CLOS Usersand Implementors Workshop

This event was held October 3rd and 4th, 1988 at Xerox Palo Alto Research Center (PARC) in Palo Alto, California. It was sponsored by the Xerox Corporation.
