# Object Technology 93 (OT93)

This was a conference on object-oriented technology organized by the
Object-Oriented Programming and Systems (OOPS) affinity group of the
British Computer Society (BCS). I believe it was held at Christ Church,
Oxford.

If memory serves, there was a debate to see who had the best
object-oriented language, and I was there to represent Common Lisp.

I remember feeling that COBOL should win the debate.  I don't have any
recollection of who actually won, but certainly the COBOL argument was
interesting. They just said they had the most users, or maybe it was
most installed cites, which back then they did. Apparently, they had
some very elegant message-passing interface bolted onto traditional
COBOL. It wasn't really the language of choice for object-oriented
programmers but the advocacy was coherent and got people to smile, and
honestly the interface described seemed credible.
 --Kent Pitman 23-Nov-2026
