This scan is of hand-written notes by me. It's pretty messy--not my
best job of note-taking. But I suppose it's as useful a snapshot of
impressions as any, so I am electing to just leave it in its fairly
raw form rather than to try to edit it. It appears to just be notes I
took at 1994 DIAC (Directions and Implications of Advanced Computing)
conference.

My possibly-faulty recollection of this event was that it was one of
the activities kickstarted by Computer Professionals for Social
Responsibility (CPSR), though in some sense CPSR was just a way of
speaking in the aggregate about numerous grassroots things that were
springing up on the issue of computer ethics. I very much miss CPSR,
which when it was dissolved (for reasons that were primarily
financial, I believe) left a gaping hole that has not really been
properly filled by other organizations since.
 --Kent Pitman 22-Nov-2026
