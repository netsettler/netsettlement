This document was fanfold printer, probably from an LA36 paper
terminal, which was a commonly available device of the late 1970s, not
from a line printer that one would submit to as a batch device. (I'm
not sure we had any of those.)

There is no date, but I would guess this was created toward the end of
the multi-year process of developing the translator, mainly because
the business with Control-S (which was unique to this translator, and
an accommodation to how tight we were on disk space) was probably a
feature added as things started to get used.

Did it take more disk space to implement and document that feature
than was ever saved by it? Oh, probably.  But it was a gesture to say
I understood the precious nature of disk space. In fact, that there is
any record at all in this repository is also a recognition of the
tight disk space. We were under constant pressure to delete things.
But especially when we moved from the Xerox Graphics Printer (XGP),
which was cool but slow, to the Dover, a laser printer from Xerox that
seemed like it was built out of spare parts from a photocopier but
which printed pages 10 times as fast, I started hardcopying things
before deleting them so that they weren't really lost. Not that we had
scanners and certainly not OCR, but in the worst case things could be
typed back in. And anyway, it was there to read.

An entire session was on a single stream of paper, and I probably had
arranged for a print command at the bottom of a page, so that the text
here would begin at the top.

You can tell this was an interactive session by the `$$U` and the
console free at the bottom. As explained in better detail in 
[the README](README.md), `$$U` was a command to log out of DDT,
the ITS interactive shell. But my point is that it wasn't part of the
instructions.
