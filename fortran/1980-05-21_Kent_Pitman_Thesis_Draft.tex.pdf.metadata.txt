This appears to be a draft of the thesis as I had begun it.

I think it has some conceptual and technical overlap with the paper
"A FORTRAN->LISP Translator" that appears in the
Proceedings of the 1979 Macsyma Users' Conference,
Washington, D.C., June 20-22, 1979.
A webbed version is on my personal web site at
https://nhplace.com/kent/Papers/Fortran-to-Lisp.html
But the thesis was of course going to be longer and able to go into
more detail.

There are separate documents in this repository, like notes on how
Fortran compiles things and how my translator did equivalent things,
that were probably being assembled by me to include in the thesis
had I continued to work on it.

The printer cover page is included to show the date, though the use of
">" in the filename meant latest version, and it seems I suppressed
headers so we can't see what version.

The directory "PIQUE" (which I intended to be pronounced "PK") was one
of my several personal directories. "PK" is my initials, backwards. I
had a lot of directories on ITS and most had some relationship to my
name, or to some project I worked on, but this one was probably one of
the more mysterious ones, so I figured it might as well be
mentioned. It was also a pun because at the time I created it, I
dating Phyllis Koton, a fellow computer science student. Her login
name on the same machine was "PK". So the naming was an homage to her
as well; the directories were homonyms.
