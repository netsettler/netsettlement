The most important thing to know about this document is that it
involves some notes I had taken about "hard cases" in translation.
This is NOT documentation on what the translator actually did.

As I recall, the translator was pretty strong on things like DATA,
COMMON, and EQUIVALENCE, or at least the architecture was designed to
make all of that very easy. I don't know that I implemented every last
syntactic feature, though.

On the other hand, if I had gotten around to making the translator
support the example in these notes where you could pass go tags to a
subroutine, I'd be surprised (but pleased).

[If I even fully understand the semantics of that last item, it feels
like it wouldn't have been hard to do, it was just an obscure special
case. Looking back on it, it looks similar to modern Common Lisp
catch/throw or restarts, though in the day I think it might have been
seen as coroutines.]
 --Kent Pitman 13-Nov-2026
