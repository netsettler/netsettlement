# HarlequinMoo (a.k.a. HarleyMoo)

Within Harlequin, we created a [MOO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MOO), a text-based virtual
reality, like an interactive or a chat room that allowed actions for exploring
and socializing, not just chat.
Within Harlequin, this allowed  for remote workers to keep in touch.

Originally, Judy Anderson and I (Kent Pitman) used it, since I worked
from home on a Unix box via a 
[SLIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serial_Line_Internet_Protocol)
connection. Later it connected (and described) a global network of offices.

In the early days, it was common for people not at the original Harlequin
home base in Cambridge, UK to visit it, just to know everyone. As the company
grew larger, that was impractical. On one visit to the UK, I collected some notes on
details of the office space that I thought would make fun elements of our MOO.
Others then furnished me with other supporting documents.
Eventually, you could virtually visit Harlequin offices globally from your desktop.

The information here is just notes I had laying around, and they may
give you a gestalt of the kind of data
one might build into a MOO. The addresses and phone numbers are no longer working.
Please do not bug people who may have them today by calling, as that company
no longer exists and all that contact info has surely been reassigned to other people by now
who would be surprised and annoyed if you tried.
