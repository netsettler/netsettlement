Please note that there are two parts to this document. If you only
look at the first few pages, you'll get confused as to its nature.
There is additional context in the README.md in this folder.

The total size of this PDF document is 198 pages.

The first part is hand-written notes by me on the meeting itself:
who said what, what kinds of votes were taken, etc.

The second part (which begins numbering as page 1 but is actually page
24 of this PDF document) is the 175 page file that I maintained called
  S:>KMP>lisp>x3j13>notes>positions.text.580
This is a file on the Stony-Brook.SCRC.Symbolics.COM file server
that Moon and I used to coherently coordinate our representation
of Symbolics positions to X3J13.

This is NOT a committee document, it was an internal communication
between me and Moon.

At the time it would have been considered confidential, but once the
standard was published, it lost any strategic power and became just
a historical artifact.

When constructing this archive, I contacted Moon to see if he minded
having this go public, since it might have personal impressions of his
somewhere in it; he said it was fine to use without that kind of
filtering.
