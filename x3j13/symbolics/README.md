# Symbolics Artifacts

This folder contains artifacts that were ***not official documents of
X3J13 but rather Symbolics-internal artifacts.***

For several years, David Moon was the Symbolics primary representative
to X3J13, and I (Kent Pitman) was the alternate representative. I
maintained an ever-growing document that was a master list of all the
cleanup issues that might come up at meetings along with information
about the history and current Symbolics position. Using this document,
either Moon or I would immediately have a sense of what our corporate
position was if something came up for vote.

There were several of the documents that contained meeting notes and
Symbolics positions. They grew in size over time. The most recent and
largest is the one for the December 1991 meeting, which was 198 pages
(23 initial pages of meeting notes and 175 pages of notes on Symbolics
positions on issues that might need to be voted).\
