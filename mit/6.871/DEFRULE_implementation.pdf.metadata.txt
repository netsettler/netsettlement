Trivia about the original paper artifact: Different from many other
 bits of hardcopy, this was printed on MIT stationary. An MIT
 watermark did not show up in the image, fortunately, as it would
 distract from the text, but a good piece of the page had a very faint
 MIT logo embedded in the sheet of paper. The text that DID scan looks
 more like it was typed in on a typewriter than printed on the normal
 XGP or Dover laser printer that were commonly used to print things in
 those days.  Maybe it was done on a home printer, but it was
 unusually good quality. (None of this matters to the content, it was
 just interesting, I thought.)
